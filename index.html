Chicken Biryani Recipe

A delicious and spicy Pakistani rice dish loved by everyone!

Homemade Chicken Biryani with aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500 grams chicken
  • 2 large onions, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoons biryani masala
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Wash the rice and soak it in water for 20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and fry the sliced onions until golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and chicken. Cook for a few minutes.
  4. Add tomatoes, yogurt, biryani masala, and salt. Cook until the chicken is tender.
  5. Boil the rice in salted water until it is about 70% cooked, then drain it.
  6. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture and add coriander and mint.
  7. Cover the pot and cook on low heat
